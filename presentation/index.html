The big idea

A premium shop with a sense of humour

What it looks like

A calm, high-end lifestyle store. Clean typography, generous space, strong product photography.

What it secretly says

The product descriptions gently roast the customer, the products, and modern marketing — without ever getting in the way.

The contrast is the brand: it looks like a shop you'd trust, and reads like a friend who's a little too honest.

Feature · The wishlist

"Your questionable taste"

  • Slides in over the shop as a side panel You never lose your place while reviewing your picks.
  • Shows each item with its photo, price, quantity, and a running total Clear at a glance — the important numbers stand out.
  • A considered empty state "Nothing here. A rare moment of financial responsibility."

Signature feature · The Honesty Toggle

Two ways to read every product

Marketing Mode

"A thoughtfully engineered ceramic vessel designed to elevate your daily coffee ritual."

Honesty Mode

"It's a mug. You already own three."

One switch flips every product between polished sales copy and the blunt truth. It's the brand's personality made interactive.

Signature feature · The Taste Report

It judges your choices

Your Questionable Taste Report

Shopping personality: The Aspiring Executive — "You have the wishlist of someone who schedules meetings about scheduling meetings."

Final verdict: "Your taste is expensive. Your commitment to buying things remains theoretical."

A playful, personalised summary of the wishlist — a shopping personality, the "most questionable decision", and a verdict. It updates live as the wishlist changes.

The choices we made

Built right-sized, not over-built

  • We kept the toolkit lean We used small, well-matched tools rather than heavy machinery the project doesn't need — faster to build, cheaper to maintain.
  • The wishlist is robust Even if saved data is corrupted or your browser blocks storage, the shop keeps working instead of breaking.
  • Nothing is faked or hardcoded Every price, total, and report figure is calculated from the real product data, so it's always consistent.

Being straight with you

What this is — and isn't (yet)

  • It's a complete, polished demo of the shopping experience Browsing, wishlist, quantities, and the two signature features all work end to end.
  • Product photos currently come from a free image library A real shop would host and optimise its own product imagery.
  • There's no checkout or payment It's a front-end experience; connecting real inventory and payments would be the next step.
